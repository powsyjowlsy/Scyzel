Institutional Philosophy

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Rooted in Catholic educational tradition and inspired by St. Catherine of Alexandria and St. Dominic de Guzman, Saint Catherine's College operates as a dynamic learning community dedicated to holistic transformation.

Institutional Vision

Our Guiding Vision

We envision St. Catherine’s College as a prominent Catholic learning center, dynamic and exemplary in providing research-skilled, competent, and God-fearing individuals ready for the world of work in the service of God, country, and community.

Institutional Mission

Our Mission Commitment

Inspired by the courage and virtues of St. Catherine of Alexandria and St. Dominic de Guzman, St. Catherine’s College is a Catholic educational institution bridging Filipino tradition and global progress, nurturing Christ-like attitudes, and committing itself to excellence in knowledge for holistic transformation.

Academic Programs

Educational Offerings from K-12 to College

Saint Catherine's College provides a continuous educational pathway accredited by DepEd, CHED, and PAASCU, designed to prepare students for college matriculation and professional leadership.

Designed specifically for students aspiring to lead enterprises, establish business ventures, or practice accounting, economics, and corporate financial administration.

Humanities and Social Sciences (HUMSS)

Geared toward students intending to pursue careers in legal practice, teacher education, public administration, psychology, communications, and social policy development.

Institutional Procedure

6-Step Higher Education Admission Guide

Follow these sequential on-campus clearance steps across institutional offices to complete collegiate admission, starting at the Registrar and Accounting service windows shown alongside.

Expand
Registrar & Cashier Hall Steps 01 and 02
01

Registrar's Office

Proceed to the Registrar's Office for initial admission assistance and submit the required admission documents for verification.

02

Accounting Office

Pay the admission examination fee amounting to ₱200.00 at Window 1.

03

Guidance Office

Present official receipt as proof of payment to schedule and take your diagnostic admission test.

04

Computer Laboratory

Take the computerized admission exam as part of the formal institutional admission requirements.

05

Wellness Center

Accomplish the Student Health Information Form for official health clearance and student medical documentation.

06

VP for Student Affairs (SAS)

Attend the final interview for comprehensive admission evaluation and formal completion of the process.

Step 1: Account Creation & Registration

Access student portal at digital-online.net/orlan_enrollment/. Select NEW for incoming freshmen/transferees, OLD for returning students.

Step 2: Admission Testing (New & Transferee Only)

If exam not yet taken, proceed to Window 1 of Accounting and pay the ₱200 exam fee. Bring receipt to Guidance Office to take test, then refresh portal.

Step 3: Information Sheet Submission

Log into your portal account and completely fill out the digital Student Information Sheet.

Step 4: Secure Medical Requirements

Proceed to the Registrar's Office to obtain your medical stub and referral slip for the required physical exam.

Step 5: Submission of Medical Requirements

Submit your completed medical documents and laboratory clearances to the Wellness Center.

Step 6: SSG Fee Payment

Proceed to the designated window to pay your Supreme Student Government (SSG) annual membership dues.

Step 7: Student ID Processing

Proceed to Computer Lab for ID photo capture (NEW: wear white collared shirt if uniform unavailable; OLD: wear school uniform).

Step 8: Enrollment Form Accomplishment

Log back into your student portal account to complete, review, and officially submit your Enrollment Form.

Step 9: Prospectus Retrieval

Go to the Registrar's Office to secure an official printed copy of your departmental academic Prospectus.

Step 10: Academic Advising & Subject Plotting

Visit your respective Department Chairman for academic advising, prerequisite evaluation, and subject plotting for the semester.

Step 11: Enrollment Fee Payment

Proceed to the Accounting Office to settle your initial downpayment and institutional enrollment fee.

Step 12: Study Load Printing

Go to the Registrar's Office to claim your officially validated, printed Study Load and class schedule.

Step 13: Final Tuition Assessment

Return to the Accounting Office with your study load for the official assessment of tuition and miscellaneous laboratory fees.

Step 14: Officially Enrolled!

You are now officially enrolled! Welcome to the St. Catherine's College family. Veritas et Excelsior!
