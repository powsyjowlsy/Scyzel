Institutional Philosophy

Vision, Mission & Dominican Foundation

Founded by Rev. Fr. Anastacio del Corro alongside the Dominican Sisters, Saint Catherine's College operates as a community of truth, prayer, study, and service.

Institutional Vision

Our Guiding Vision

Saint Catherine's College envisions itself as a premier Dominican Catholic educational community in southern Cebu, dynamically forming intellectually competent, morally upright, and socially committed individuals who embody Gospel values and actively contribute to the sustainable development of church and society.

Institutional Mission

Our Mission Commitments

Inspired by Saint Catherine of Siena and Saint Dominic de Guzman, we commit ourselves to:

  • Provide transformative, accessible, and learner-centered academic curricula from Basic Education to Higher Education.
  • Instill profound Christian values, ethical discipline, and social responsibility across all programs.
  • Cultivate research culture, environmental stewardship, disaster preparedness, and critical inquiry.
  • Engage the community through sustained pastoral outreach, civic involvement, and professional leadership.

The Three Dominican Core Pillars

Pillar I Veritas

Pursuit of Truth (Veritas)

Upholding uncompromised academic honesty, scientific rigor, and spiritual contemplation to discern reality in service to human dignity.

Pillar II Caritas

Universal Charity (Caritas)

Manifesting compassionate solidarity with the marginalized, fostering empathy, community stewardship, and active civic service in Carcar City.

Pillar III Scientia

Academic Mastery (Scientia)

Striving for professional competence, modern digital literacy, and holistic technical mastery across all grade levels and disciplines.

Academic Programs

Educational Offerings from K-12 to College

Saint Catherine's College provides a continuous educational pathway accredited by DepEd, CHED, and PAASCU, designed to prepare students for college matriculation and professional leadership.

SHS Academic Track Grades 11–12

Accountancy, Business and Management (ABM)

Designed specifically for students aspiring to lead enterprises, establish business ventures, or practice accounting, economics, and corporate financial administration.

Specialized Curriculum Focus:
  • Fundamentals of Accountancy, Business & Management 1 & 2
  • Business Finance, Applied Economics & Principles of Marketing
  • Business Enterprise Simulation & Commercial Ethics
Department: Senior High School DepEd Recognized
SHS Academic Track Grades 11–12

Humanities and Social Sciences (HUMSS)

Geared toward students intending to pursue careers in legal practice, teacher education, public administration, psychology, communications, and social policy development.

Specialized Curriculum Focus:
  • Philippine Politics, Governance & Constitutional Law
  • Disciplines and Ideas in Social Sciences & Applied Social Work
  • Community Engagement, Solidarity & Citizenship Practicum
Department: Senior High School DepEd Recognized
SHS Academic & Tech-Voc Grades 11–12

STEM, GAS & Technical-Vocational (TVL)

Comprehensive alternative tracks catering to future scientists, engineers, medical practitioners, and TESDA-certified technical specialists in ICT and Home Economics.

Available Strands:
  • Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM)
  • General Academic Strand (GAS)
  • TVL Information & Communications Technology (ICT)
Department: Senior High School TESDA / DepEd Aligned
College Department 4-Year Degree

College of Teacher Education (BEEd / BSEd)

Accredited undergraduate programs producing certified, values-driven pedagogical leaders equipped for elementary and secondary classroom excellence.

Degree Concentrations:
  • Bachelor of Elementary Education (BEEd)
  • Bachelor of Secondary Education (BSEd) - Major in English
  • Bachelor of Secondary Education (BSEd) - Major in Science & Math
Credential: CHED Recognized Licensure Ready

College of Business Administration (BSBA)
